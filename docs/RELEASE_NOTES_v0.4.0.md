# DeepNode v0.4.0 — large maps: streamed 1 km² region, coarse and packed terrains (2026-11-03)

## New
- **Large map support (BeamNG.earth, huge_desert, hw550).** On terrains over 16 km² the mod
  works in a 1 km × 1 km region around the player instead of the whole map. When you drive
  250 m off its centre, the region follows you: the next region loads in the background over a
  few frames, the overlap keeps its ruts, and the ground you left gets its original height back.
  Start time and memory no longer grow with the map size (about 1 MB per km² instead of up to
  268 MB per terrain array on a 16384² map). Settings: `region` in `settings/deepnode/config.json`
  (`enabled` 'auto' / true / false, `size` 1000 m, `shiftAt` 250 m).
- **Coarse terrains** (squares bigger than 2 m, e.g. huge_desert's 64 m squares): the mod works
  on its own 1 m grid over them, so ruts and mud look as on normal maps.
- Terrain files up to 32768 × 32768.

## Fixed
- **Game hang on huge_desert** (Windows "STATUS_APPLICATION_HANG"): each rut tile was one 64 m
  square with 320 × 320 cells, and the start-up pass lowered 67 million vertices (28 s).
- **Region loads on packed levels** (terrain inside a zip) stalled 0.7–1.5 s, because seeking in
  a packed file decompresses it; big packed terrains now read their layers from the engine.
- **Mud material on huge_desert** drew a rough, dark patch: terrain layer strengths are now
  clamped to 0–1, terrain textures the terrain itself leaves out (wrong size for the level's
  texture set) are left out of the mud too, and normal maps are weighted like the terrain's.
- Grass blinking next to digging in heavy physics is about halved, and vehicles near grass no
  longer ride on the old terrain for up to 1 s.
- Decal road copies upload 23 % fewer corners (merged planar cells).

## Measured
| Map | Start | Region move (load / switch frame) | Driving |
|---|---|---|---|
| huge_desert (8192² × 64 m, ~275k km², packed) | 0.8 s (was 30 s, then hang) | 14–31 ms over 4–7 frames / 17–59 ms | ruts at 58–60 fps, mod ≤ 8.5 ms per frame |
| hw550 (8192² × 1 m, 67 km²) | 1.35 s | 9–15 ms over 3–4 frames / 4–12 ms | — |
| gridmap_v2 forced to a 1 km region | — | 8–14 ms over 3–4 frames / 4–13 ms | ruts kept across moves, exact terrain restore |
| gridmap_v2 (4 km², whole map as before) | 0.26–0.34 s | — | same 9673 sunk vertices, rut floor works |

The switch frame on 8192² terrains is mostly the engine's own terrain update (15–60 ms per call
on maps that size). No 4/8-truck fps benchmark was run for this release.

## Known issues
- Ruts outside the region overlap are dropped when the region moves (they are at least 250 m
  behind you); AI vehicles outside the region leave no ruts.
- Coarse terrains: the terrain can only be lowered per real vertex, so within one square (64 m on
  huge_desert) of hard ground, roads or the region edge the ruts stay hidden under the terrain;
  vehicles sink by the lowering, not into the ruts (no rut floor there). Mud 80–150 m away reads
  a little lighter than the terrain (the terrain fades its textures with distance).
- `roads.mode` 'drape' keeps the roads draped as at start in region mode.
- Not yet tried on a map generated by BeamNG.earth.
- See docs/OPEN_ISSUES.md for the full list.

## Install
Download `DeepNode-v0.4.0.zip` and drop it, unextracted, into
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\BeamNG\BeamNG.drive\current\mods\`. Remove the older DeepNode zip first.
